# Jet-engine recovery workspace

Mode: Operate. Buyer/maintenance engineering staff compare source-backed recovery options at a desk; mobile preserves inspection and review access.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Make the physical assembly the entry point into an auditable sourcing decision. Every animated part leads to its own evidence and case.

OWN-WORLD: Warm off-white work surfaces, deep forest green navigation/actions, pale sage selection, restrained amber unknowns. Self-hosted Geist, compact numeric tables, plain citations and thin separators.

STORY: Upload a blueprint and part requirements, confirm cited extraction, validate a generated assembly, choose a part, investigate suppliers, compare evidence/cost, review a versioned approval draft.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Sources use a left upload column and right engine concept image. Workspace gives the real mesh most of the width with a selected-part inspector to the right. Direct separation controls and the three available component identities sit below the scene. Narrow screens stack the inspector and retain keyboard controls.

FORM: Assembly-first workspace, selected autonomously from the code-led Operate direction after Yixin asked for ideation in Figma. Concept seed reference: seed:ec51802aa826a144. Seven illustrative module groups contain three selectable manifest identities.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: Anime.js scroll synchronizes explosion and reverses on upward scroll; R3F renders real geometry. Clicking a mesh/pin/list item opens its case. Actual server events drive research; animation never claims engineering compatibility. Reduced motion and a direct slider bypass scroll animation.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
