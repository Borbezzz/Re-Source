## Visual authority and interpretation

Quiet premium industrial software: charcoal background, offwhite type, warm-gray secondary text, restrained spacing, hairline dividers, contextual controls. NASA Eyes, Shapr3D and Vizcom inform spatial interaction; Esri informs map/detail/filtering; Celonis informs visible checks; Palantir informs disruption decisions; Zip informs procurement; Xometry informs realistic inputs. The exact sources and what to borrow are in DESIGN-FLOW.md. These are pattern references, not endorsements or dependencies.

The rejected oversized-slogan/engine-render first screen is deliberately excluded. `01-design-package-v2.png` is the approved entry reference.

For this presentation redesign, this approved package supersedes conflicting prior visual/navigation instructions. Existing backend/data/security contracts remain authoritative until a separately scoped change is implemented and verified. Do not read this as a wholesale backend rewrite, removal of other working product flows, or permission to overwrite a teammate's active work.
